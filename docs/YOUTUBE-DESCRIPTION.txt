--- LONG DESCRIPTION (main video) ---

Chip Slicer Hierarchy turns filtering in Power BI into a chip/pill slicer that drills
down. Drop up to three fields into one well and the field order becomes the hierarchy:
click a chip to filter, expand it to browse the level below without filtering.

No separate well per level, and no second visual to show the next level.

WHAT YOU SEE IN THIS VIDEO
Setting it up in a real report, the hierarchy expanding and filtering as you click, and
the two features a Pro licence adds: search across the whole hierarchy, and a value
heatmap that colours each chip by its measure.

WHAT IT DOES
• Up to three hierarchy levels (Category → SubCategory → Product) from a single well
• Click a chip to filter; expand to browse a level without filtering
• Multi-select, leaf-only selection and auto-collapse of sibling branches
• An image per chip and a value badge (compact / number / currency / percent)
• Independent colour sets per level: inactive, active and parent-of-selection
• Cross-filtering, cross-highlighting and bookmarks
• Keyboard activation and high-contrast support

FREE vs PRO
The slicer is complete without a licence. Pro adds two things: finding what you need in
a large hierarchy, and seeing where the weight is.

Free:
• All three hierarchy levels, up to 2,000 values
• Multi-select and leaf-only selection
• Custom per-level colours
• Auto-collapse siblings, reset button and 'All' chip
• Image chips, value badges and tooltips
• Cross-filtering, cross-highlighting, bookmarks, keyboard and high contrast

Pro:
• Search box — filter chips across the whole hierarchy as you type, with the matching
  text marked inside each chip and the number of results shown
• Value heatmap — each chip tinted by its measure, scaled level by level so a child is
  compared with its siblings rather than with the top of the hierarchy

Turn either on without a licence and Power BI shows its own notification with the link
to obtain one. The setting is kept and applies as soon as the licence is active.
30-day free trial on AppSource.

PRIVACY
No network calls of any kind. Images must be embedded in your model as Base64 — external
URLs are rejected before they are ever loaded, which is what makes that claim literally
true.
