--- LONG DESCRIPTION (main video) ---

Chip Slicer Hierarchy is a chip slicer for Power BI that drills down. Drop up to three
fields into one well and the field order becomes the hierarchy: click a chip to filter,
open it to see the level below. The filter applies to the deepest level you selected.

One visual instead of three linked slicers.

WHAT IT DOES
• Up to three levels (Category → Subcategory → Product) from a single well
• Click a chip to filter; the arrow opens a level without filtering
• Multi-select, leaf-only selection and auto-collapse of sibling branches
• Default selection by values (e.g. "Spain, France"), applied when the page opens
• Six style templates: Classic, Outline, Soft, Square, One colour, Dark
• An image per chip and a value badge with the measure's own format
• Round ✕ to clear, an "All" chip, on-object formatting
• Sync slicers, bookmarks, edit interactions and cross-highlighting
• Keyboard: one Tab stop, arrow keys, Right/Left to open and close a level
• 12 languages

FREE AND PRO
The slicer is complete without a licence. Pro adds three features.

Free:
• Up to three levels and 2,000 rows
• Images, value badges, tooltips
• Multi-select, leaf-only selection, default selection, ✕ and All
• Six templates, colours per level, on-object formatting
• Keyboard, 12 languages, sync slicers and bookmarks

Pro:
• Search box — finds chips at every level as you type, marks the matching text and
  shows the number of results
• Colour by value — each chip tinted by its measure, scaled level by level so a child is
  compared with its siblings
• Drop-down mode — the hierarchy folds into a bar that shows the selection and opens the
  tree on click

Without a licence you can try them while editing: they are drawn working under a
"Pro preview" watermark, and Power BI shows its own notice with the link to obtain a
licence. Readers of the report see the free slicer.

PRIVACY
No network calls, no telemetry, no cookies. Images must be embedded in your model as
Base64 — web addresses are not loaded.
